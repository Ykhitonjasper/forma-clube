# Research — FormaClube

Audience: someone who shares a community kiln and needs the wet pot's numbers before loading day. They do not run a shop, and they do not want a browser tab covered in clay.

## What people already use

- ClayCalc (claycalc.com) is a free set of web calculators: shrinkage, target finished size, clay weight, kiln loading, cone temperatures. Nothing is saved against a named piece, and the page assumes you are online at the bench.
- HQ Calculator's kiln shelf planner takes kiln interior size and one piece size, then prints pieces per shelf. One anonymous load, no clay card, no history of last month's mugs.
- Creek Road Pottery's cost app is a studio business tool: bag prices, glaze costing, energy per firing, a visual kiln planner. Built for a potter running the books, not for a Tuesday-night member who wants to know if a wall is too thin.
- A community-studio thread on shrinkage (July 2024) lands on the same practical answer: stoneware is about 12%, the bag's plus-or-minus 2% will not be tamed by kiln position, and people still throw a size ladder because they cannot remember last week's wet diameter.

## Gap this app fills

A phone book for one shared kiln. Pick the clay the club stocks, type the wall or the wet diameter or the glaze pint weight, and keep that reading on the piece. Shelves in the kiln are named places, so a packing note can point at Shelf B instead of "somewhere in the middle." No account, no camera, no live location. The clay cards and glaze batches are seeded from bodies and mixes a community studio actually stocks, and a member can save another reading on top.

## Neighbours in this series

The last closed slot was a still editor for print sizes. The slot before that was a home food cupboard. This one is a bench book for wet clay and a shared kiln. Different job, different screens.
