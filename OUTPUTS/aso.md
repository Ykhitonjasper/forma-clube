## Description
FormaClube is the bench book for a shared kiln. Needle a wall, measure a wet diameter, weigh a glaze pint, and check whether the piece fits the posts on Shelf B. Clay cards carry shrink, a wall range, and how long that body takes to reach leather. Glaze batches are written in grams with the pint weight that keeps them in band. Past loads say what happened the last time that shelf was full.

Everything stays on the phone. There is no account and nothing to sync.
